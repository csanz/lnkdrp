## Breaking and behaviour changes, read these first

1. **Free view emails no longer link to the reader page.** That page is Pro-only and answered
   "No reader by that id" from the button in the mail (review M24). On Free the primary button
   goes to the document's metrics page (`/doc/:id/metrics?shareId=...`) and the secondary
   "See who opened it" to `/pricing?from=view_email`. Pro emails are unchanged.
   `src/lib/notifications/viewNotifications.ts`, `tests/lib/viewNotifications.test.ts`.
2. **`/preferences` redirects to the dashboard.** The standalone copy of the account, workspace,
   usage and billing tabs is deleted (900 lines that had drifted; the infinite invite refetch of
   review H8 lived only there). `/preferences?tab=x` and `/preferences/x` go to
   `/dashboard?tab=x` (`spending` maps to `billing`, unknown tabs to `account`); the fragment in
   old view emails (`#email-preferences`) survives the redirect. `src/app/preferences/page.tsx`,
   `[tab]/page.tsx`, `tabs.ts`; `src/app/preferences/WorkspaceManager.tsx` and
   `preferencesShell.tsx` removed.
3. **MCP confirmation policy for destructive tools** (`delete_doc`, `archive_doc`,
   `delete_share_link`, `delete_project`, `delete_project_link`). A prompt that fails to reach a
   human (timeout, transport error, or an instant `cancel`) now refuses the call whatever
   `confirm` says, returns the preview, and remembers the failure for ten minutes; only the next
   call for the same action may carry `confirm: true`. A pre-set `confirm: true` on the first
   call is never honoured on a client that declared elicitation. A human's decline is final.
   Before this a cancel or timeout let a pre-set flag through (review M14). Preview headlines and
   facts are now sanitised like every other document-derived string (M15). `mcp/src/confirm.ts`,
   `docs/MCP.md` "Confirmation", `tests/lib/mcpConfirm.test.ts`.
4. **Inline upload limits over MCP.** `fileBase64` and `filePath` on `lnkdrp_share_pdf` and
   `lnkdrp_replace_pdf` accept at most **16 MB** before optimisation (was 50 MB, refused from the
   base64 length before decoding), and against a hosted API what is sent after optimisation must
   be under **3 MB** or the tool answers `too_large` naming the size and pointing at `sourceUrl`
   (which keeps the 50 MB document ceiling). The `/mcp` body limit is 24 MB with a JSON-RPC 413,
   and the bearer check runs before the body is buffered (review H4: an anonymous 66 MB POST used
   to be parsed first). Ghostscript and pdfjs run at most two at a time. `mcp/src/inlineLimits.ts`,
   `semaphore.ts`, `optimize.ts`, `main.ts`.
5. **MCP session caps.** 20 live sessions per credential (the stalest is closed to admit a new
   one) and 500 per process (`initialize` answers JSON-RPC 429). `mcp/src/sessionCaps.ts`,
   `LNKDRP_MCP_MAX_SESSIONS_PER_KEY`, `LNKDRP_MCP_MAX_SESSIONS`. Idempotency replays are now
   scoped per credential (200 entries each, 5,000 in total) so one busy key cannot evict
   another's (`mcp/src/idempotency.ts`).
6. **There is no personal workspace, only workspaces.** The one an account starts with is a
   workspace like any other. The Free cap is **two owned workspaces, whichever they are**
   (`FREE_WORKSPACES = 2` in `src/lib/billing/planLimits.ts`, replacing "one team workspace beside
   the personal one"; the `team_workspaces` limit key keeps its name); invites work in every
   workspace, gated by the collaborator limit alone; a share page signs with the workspace's
   name, falling back to the owner only while the name is still the placeholder
   (`src/lib/orgs/defaultName.ts`); the MCP connection name is `lnkdrp-<workspace>` in every case
   (`lnkdrp-personal` only while the workspace is still called Personal). Every screen and help
   article that said "personal" or "team workspace" now says "workspace". Commits `f0216f2`,
   `e867788`, `30031d4`.
7. **A recipient's upload secret is read-only once the upload completes** (review H1). A
   recipient who kept the secret from a request or replace link could mint a new Blob token and
   swap the bytes of a completed version in place, with no new version, no compare and no email.
   The Blob token route, `PATCH /api/uploads/:id` and `POST /api/uploads/:id/process` each match
   the upload's status in their own filter; a completed row answers the same 404/403 as a wrong
   secret. `GET` keeps working for the recipient's page. `src/lib/uploads/secretAuth.ts`,
   `tests/upload/secretAuth.test.ts`.
8. **Project names and slugs are unique among live projects only.** A soft-deleted project no
   longer holds its name (the old index answered 409 for a project nobody could see).
   `db/migration/20260925_0003`, `src/lib/models/Project.ts`.
9. **AI run records expire after 30 days** (`AI_RUN_RETENTION_DAYS`). They hold prompt text built
   from customers' documents and were kept forever. Existing rows older than the retention are
   removed when the index builds. `src/lib/models/AiRun.ts`, `db/migration/20260925_0004`.
10. **`analytics-reconcile` is bounded.** The nightly counter pass covers links with activity in
    the last two days (`?days=N`; `?full=1` for everything) and holds the cron lease; it used to
    scan `shareviews` and `sharevisits` end to end (review M9). `?dryRun=1` on any cron no longer
    overwrites the job's health record (M12, `src/lib/cron/health.ts`). `usage-agg-reconcile`
    recomputes each billing cycle over all its rows, so a one-day run no longer replaces a cycle
    total with one day's spend (H6, `src/lib/usage/reconcile.ts`).
11. **Deleting a workspace or an account now touches Stripe.** `DELETE /api/orgs/:orgId` cancels
    the workspace's subscription immediately (prorated on yearly) before deleting anything and
    refuses with 502 `STRIPE_CANCEL_FAILED` if Stripe cannot be reached; `POST /api/account/delete`
    sets cancel-at-period-end on every subscription nobody else could manage (and refuses the same
    way), and where another owner or admin remains posts a `plan.subscription_ending` row asking
    them to replace the card. The 30-day purge skips a subscription already set to end, so a
    prepaid year is never forfeited. Review H2, M1; `src/lib/billing/stripeSubscriptionCancel.ts`,
    `src/app/api/account/delete/route.ts`, `src/app/api/orgs/[orgId]/route.ts`,
    `src/lib/accounts/purge.ts`, `docs/SUBSCRIPTION.md`.
12. **`/api/debug` and `/api/debug/cookie` are admin-only** through `requireAdmin` (404 to anyone
    else in production). A preview host used to hand a session cookie back to any signed-in user.
    `tests/lib/debugRoutesAdmin.test.ts`.
13. **Contained documents are absent from every workspace-wide list.** New field
    `Doc.visibility: "workspace" | "project"`; nothing existing changes (the default is
    `workspace`), but any listing built outside the shared filter would miss contained documents,
    and `tests/lib/containedDocListings.test.ts` pins every listing to it. Details under owners.
14. **A locked data room answers 401 for every document id** on
    `POST /api/share/:shareId/download-requests` and on the stats route; it used to answer 404 or
    401 by membership, which leaked which ids were inside (review M8, `docs/SECURITY.md` 7.8).
15. **The activity feed hides instrumentation rows** (`funnel.*`, `checkout.started`) and the
    feature-gate refusals (`plan.limit_reached` on `version_history`, `analytics_history`,
    `project_links`) unless a `type=` filter names them; the counted caps stay visible.
    `plan.limit_reached` is written once per workspace and limit per ten minutes, so the admin
    overview counts slightly fewer rows on hot paths. `src/lib/activity/feedVisibility.ts`,
    `planLimitResponse` in `src/lib/billing/planLimits.ts`.
16. **Stripe webhook, yearly subscriptions.** When the licensed item is yearly the handler nulls
    `stripeSubscriptionItemId` and turns on-demand off, so a row switched to yearly in the Stripe
    dashboard stops allocating credits no metered item bills. No new webhook events.
17. Smaller ones: `User.tempSecretHash` is `select: false` (every read must ask for it);
    `GET /api/docs/:id/changes` is read-only for viewers and API keys; `GET /api/admin/data/requests`
    no longer runs a backfill on every load (it moved to migration `20260925_0005`); the MCP's
    `get_share_link_password` refusal no longer blames "an API key" when the caller signed in over
    OAuth (`api_key_forbidden` code unchanged); `activityevents` reads for the funnel use a new
    `{ type, createdDate }` index.

## For owners and members

**Slack integration** (new, every plan; `docs/prds/lnkdrp-slack.md`, help at `/help/slack`).
**Integrations** is a new sidebar entry; `/integrations/slack` connects channels with Slack's own
install screen (`incoming-webhook` scope, private channels work). Five moments post: a recipient's
first open (and their introduction), the visit brief, a replaced document with its change
summary or a new share link, a file received in a request inbox, and a new document (finished
uploading, or added to a data room). Each channel has five switches, "Send a test message" and
"Disconnect"; the first channel is the default and projects can be routed to their own channel
(one project posts to one channel). Reader identity follows the plan as in the emails: Pro names
the reader, Free reads "Someone". Posts are queued in `SlackOutbox`, sent at the moment, retried by
the `notification-emails` cron on the email backoff ladder, capped at 30 a minute per channel with
one "and N more" line, and a webhook Slack reports gone marks the channel Disconnected. Webhook
URLs are stored encrypted. Off entirely until the deployment has `SLACK_CLIENT_ID` and
`SLACK_CLIENT_SECRET` (the page says so). Activity rows `integration.slack_connected` and
`integration.slack_disconnected`. `src/lib/slack/*`, `src/app/(app)/integrations/*`,
`src/app/api/slack/*`, `src/app/api/orgs/active/slack/*`.

**A document's home project, and staying inside it** (`docs/prds/lnkdrp-project-home.md`).
- Upload into a data room: the upload page has an "Add to a data room" picker (preselected by
  `/upload?project=<id>`, where a project page's new "Upload here" button goes); `POST /api/docs`
  takes `projectId`. The document is in the room from its first write, so there is one creation
  event ("... in Data room" in the feed, one Slack post to the room's channel). A request inbox is
  refused (400 `PROJECT_IS_INBOX`).
- Contained documents: "Only inside this data room" at upload, or "Keep inside its data room" from
  the document menu, keeps a document out of the Docs sidebar, `/api/docs`, search, tags, starred,
  recent changes, the dashboard, workspace metrics and suggested documents; its activity rows show
  under the project's feed; Slack routes it to the room's channel or nowhere. Its direct link,
  metrics, history and links keep working. A contained document refuses a second project
  (409 `CONTAINED`). The document page shows a **Contained** pill; `doc.contained` and
  `doc.uncontained` feed rows. `src/lib/docs/visibility.ts`.

**A data-room reader is named everywhere.** A reader who introduces themselves on a room's landing
page was "Someone" in visit briefs, Slack posts and on the reader page while the feed named them.
One helper (`src/lib/share/readerIdentity.ts`) reads the arrival row and the share views the way
the feed does; the brief row stores the name; the introduction itself posts to Slack as its own

**Analytics on Free show real numbers instead of blurred rows.** The metrics page's locked block now

button, computed over the document's whole life (`src/lib/analytics/teaser.ts`; the document and
project share-views routes send `teaser` on the basic tier). Identities were always recorded on
Free and are revealed on upgrade; the teaser says how many there are. The reader page on Free
shows the upgrade block instead of "No reader by that id".

**Every credit and plan wall points at the door that opens.** The out-of-credits modal, the blocked
banner, the sidebar credits row and the Overview credits card offer the cheapest credit pack first
on Free and Pro second; yearly Pro (no on-demand) is routed to `/credits` for packs. The

`/credits` "Or get Pro" card offers the Monthly / Yearly choice (hidden once on Pro). The home
upload notice shows the grace countdown; document-cap copy reads the real constant. Commit
`4c7e571`, `99a7d2c`.

**Pages paint from what the tab already knows.** Metrics, Activity, Search, Requests and Tags show

sidebar snapshot already says there is nothing (`src/lib/client/pageCache.ts`, `knownEmpty.ts`);
Activity paints "No activity yet" from a new `activity.any` flag on the sidebar snapshot. The

## For agents over MCP

- **Connect by signing in (OAuth 2.1), keys unchanged.** Any client that implements MCP
  authorization (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, the Claude.ai and ChatGPT
  connectors) adds `https://mcp.lnkdrp.com/mcp` with no key: the MCP's resource metadata names the
  app as authorization server, the app registers the client (RFC 7591, `POST /api/oauth/register`,
  20 an hour per address), the person picks a workspace and clicks Allow at `/connect/authorize`,
  and the client exchanges a PKCE-bound code for an `lnko_` access token (1 hour) and an `lnkr_`
  refresh token (30 days). `verifyBearer` resolves the token like a key to the same `Actor`, so
  every tool, gate, plan limit and activity row is unchanged; the MCP binds the session to the
  grant id (`credentialId`), so the hourly refresh keeps the session. Grants list on `/connect`
  beside keys as "Signed in" with one Revoke (`DELETE /api/agent/keys/:id` takes either id;
  `POST /api/oauth/revoke` too). A viewer's grant is read-only. The `/mcp/<client>` guides and
  `/connect` lead with signing in; Grok keeps the key steps. `src/lib/agents/oauth.ts`,
  `oauthAuthorize.ts`, `oauthHttp.ts`, models `OAuthClient`, `OAuthCode`, `OAuthGrant`,
  `src/app/api/oauth/*`, `src/app/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server/route.ts`,
  `docs/MCP.md` "Signing in instead of a key", `docs/prds/lnkdrp-mcp-oauth.md`. Not yet run
  against a live client.
- **Four new tools, 37 in all** (`docs/MCP.md`):
  - `lnkdrp_list_revisions { docId? | shareId?, since?, limit?, cursor? }`: every replacement's
    change record, newest first, workspace-wide or for one document, `since` as an ISO date or
    `24h`, `7d`, `30d`, `this_week`, `this_month`; keyset-paginated.
  - `lnkdrp_get_revision { docId? | shareId?, version?, includeText? }`: the full compare for one
    version (summary, itemised changes, per-page change kind with previous and new wording, file
    sizes and page counts, the compare's own state, optionally both versions' text).
  - `lnkdrp_revision_contributors { docId? | shareId?, since? }`: who makes the changes, per member
    and per agent client, most active first.
  - `lnkdrp_set_doc_visibility { docId, visibility }`: keep a document inside its data room or list
    it in the workspace again; `VISIBILITY_NEEDS_PROJECT` (400) when it is in no project.
  Behind them: `GET /api/changes` (workspace-wide, `?since=`, `?docId=`, `?contributors=1`,
  cursor) and `?version=N` on `GET /api/docs/:id/changes`; `PATCH /api/docs/:id { visibility }`.
- `lnkdrp_share_pdf` takes `projectId` or `projectSlug` (at most one) and creates the document
  inside that room from the first write; the project is resolved before anything is created.
  `lnkdrp_list_docs` and `lnkdrp_get_share` carry `primaryProjectId` and `visibility`;
  `lnkdrp_list_docs` leaves contained documents out (find them through `lnkdrp_get_project`);
  `lnkdrp_add_docs_to_project` reports refused contained documents under `contained`.
- `lnkdrp_whoami` (and `GET /api/agent/whoami`) reports `credentialId`, `credentialKind`
  (`key` | `oauth`) and `integrations.slack` (channels, default, routing, switches; never the
  webhook, and no tool writes it). `keyPrefix` reads "signed in" for a grant.
- The confirmation policy (change 3), inline limits (4), session caps and per-credential
  idempotency (5) above. `/healthz` now reports `sessionCaps` and `confirmations: "enforced"`.
- CORS on `/mcp` is an origin allow-list (`MCP_CORS_ORIGINS`), off by default; the OAuth endpoints
  and metadata documents are CORS-open.
- `lnkdrp_get_activity` accepts the new types (`funnel.*`, `checkout.started`, `doc.contained`,
  `doc.uncontained`, `integration.slack_*`, `agent.authorized`, `agent.disconnected`,
  `share.visit_briefed`).
- The e2e harness (`tests/mcp/e2e.ts`) survives a Free workspace, asserts the revision tools and
  cleans up its tags; the 2026-09-24 runs are recorded in `docs/reviews/mcp-test-run-2026-09-24.md`
  (58/58 on Pro over OAuth, 50/50 on Free).
