## Where to look

- **The document page** has a quick stats card with totals, the top links and the most recently opened links.
- **The metrics page** for a document (Metrics in the document header) has the full picture: totals, charts and, on Pro, the viewer list.
- **Each link** has its own counts in the links list, so you can compare audiences.
- **Activity** in the sidebar lists every open and download as it happens.

## What is counted

- **Views**: the number of recipients who opened the link.
- **Opens**: sittings. A reader who came back three times is one view and three opens.
- **Unique viewers**: the number of different people in the period.
- **Pages viewed** and **time spent**: how far people got and how long they stayed. Only time with the tab in the foreground counts.
- **Downloads**: PDF downloads, when you allow them.
- **Views by day**: a chart of opens over the period.

Your own opens, and those of your teammates, are excluded from every figure. If you want to check your own link, sign in first so the visit is recognised as yours.

## Free: basic analytics

Free workspaces see the last 7 days: totals, the views-by-day chart, total time on the document and a count of unique viewers. The metrics page shows "N people viewed this document in the last 7 days" without saying who.

## Pro: deep analytics

Pro workspaces see everything, with full history:

- Who opened the document: the name or email a viewer gave, or their lnkdrp account if they were signed in.
- Per viewer: pages seen, time on each page, downloads, and first and last seen.
- Visits: each sitting separately, with time per page, revisited pages and the reading order.
- Return visits, so you can tell a first look from a third.
- Every link's own viewers.

Viewer identities are recorded on Free too; they are only withheld. Upgrading reveals them for past visits as well.

## Introduce yourself

Recipients can optionally give a name and email in the viewer, or in the header of a data room. When they do, their activity is labelled with it on your side, and this is shown on Free as well as Pro, because they volunteered it to you. A signed-in viewer's account identity is used instead. Recipients can decline.

## What is not tracked

- Viewers' IP addresses are kept for security and abuse prevention only. They are never shown to you.
- No device fingerprinting, and no third-party analytics or advertising trackers anywhere.
- Nothing a viewer types into the document is recorded. The viewer is read-only.
- Owner and teammate previews are excluded from every count.

See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the full list of what is recorded about viewers.

## Bots and crawlers

Share pages carry a no-index instruction, and lnkdrp's robots rules tell crawlers not to fetch share links at all. This keeps your document out of search engines and keeps bot hits out of your view counts. The rules are advisory: a crawler that ignores them could still register a view.

## Where analytics go when things change

- Disabling or deleting a link keeps its past views in the document totals.
- Archiving a document keeps its analytics; they are back when you unarchive it.
- Replacing the PDF keeps the history under the same document.
