## What lnkdrp does

lnkdrp turns a PDF into a share link. You upload the file once, send the link, and see who opened it, which pages they read and how long they stayed. Your AI agent can do the same through the lnkdrp MCP server. See [Connect your agent](/help/connect-your-agent).

Only PDFs can be shared today.

## Sign in

- Go to lnkdrp.com and click **Get Started** or **Log In**.
- Sign-in is through Google only. There is no password to set.
- During early access, new accounts may be placed on a waiting list. You are emailed when your account is ready.
- The first time you get in, you name your workspace and choose how you want to hear about visits. You can change both later in the dashboard.

## Upload a PDF

- Click **Upload** in the left sidebar.
- Choose a PDF. A preview appears in the browser. Nothing is uploaded until you click **Upload**.
- You can also import a PDF from a URL instead of choosing a file.
- Processing takes a moment: the text is extracted, a preview image is made and an AI summary with key points is written. The document status moves from preparing to ready.

The automatic summary costs 1 credit. Every Free workspace starts with 100 credits, one time. See [Credits and plans](/help/credits-and-plans).

## Get the share link

- Every document gets a default share link the moment it is created. Copy it from the document page.
- The link works right away, even while the file is still processing.
- Password protection and download control are available on every plan.
- You can add more links to the same document, one per audience, each with its own settings and stats. See [Share links](/help/share-links).

## Send it

Paste the link into an email or a message. Recipients do not need an account. The link opens in the lnkdrp viewer on any device, with the summary and key points shown first.

## What you see first

- **The document page** shows the status, a preview, the links on the document and a quick stats card.
- **The metrics page** for each document shows views, downloads and views by day. See [Analytics](/help/analytics).
- **The dashboard Overview** shows pages viewed, new documents and share views for the workspace, a 30-day activity graph and a plan card with your current usage.
- **Activity** in the sidebar is a feed of everything that happens in the workspace: uploads, link changes, opens and downloads.
- By default you get a daily digest email when recipients open your links. See [Notifications and visit briefs](/help/notifications-and-visit-briefs).

## Personal and team workspaces

Every account has a personal workspace. You can also create a team workspace and invite other people to it. Each workspace has its own documents, projects, plan, credits and agent keys, and is billed separately.

- Create one from **Dashboard**, then **Workspace**, then **Create workspace**.
- On Free, you can own one team workspace in addition to your personal one.
- Switch between workspaces from the workspace name at the top left of the app or from the dashboard.

See [Teams and workspaces](/help/teams-and-workspaces) for roles and invitations, and [Pricing](/pricing) for what each plan includes.
