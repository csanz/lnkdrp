## The chat bubble

The quickest way to reach us is the chat bubble in the app.

- **Signed in**: the chat already knows who you are. There is no email step, and your past conversations are there.
- **Signed out**: open the chat from any support link, such as Talk to us on the pricing page. You enter your email and confirm it with a code we send you, then the conversation continues.

Chat and email land in the same queue, so use whichever suits you.

## Email

Write to **support@lnkdrp.com**. Every support link in the product falls back to this address when the chat is not available.

## The support page

If we reply after you have closed the chat, our email links to [/support](/support). That page opens the chat for you so you are back in the conversation in one click, whether or not you are signed in. It also shows the email address in case the chat does not open.

## Response time

We reply within a business day.

## What to include

The more of this you include, the faster we can help:

- **The workspace name**, as shown at the top left of the app. Most things are per workspace, including plans, credits and agent keys.
- **The link URL** or document title, if the question is about a share link, a viewer or analytics.
- **The time** it happened, with your time zone.
- **What you expected** and what you saw instead. A screenshot helps.
- **Which client**, if your agent is involved: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok or another. Never paste a full agent key; the first characters are enough, and you can revoke and recreate it at any time.
- **The email address** you sign in with, if you write from a different one.

## Common questions we can answer faster

- Plans, credits and billing: see [Credits and plans](/help/credits-and-plans) first. Invoices and cancellation are in the Stripe billing portal, opened from **Manage subscription** on the dashboard.
- Why someone could not open a link: check whether the link is enabled, expired or archived in the links list. See [Share links](/help/share-links).
- Why a name is missing from analytics: viewer identities are a Pro feature unless the viewer introduced themselves. See [Analytics](/help/analytics).
- Agent connection problems: see the troubleshooting section of [Connect your agent](/help/connect-your-agent).

## Privacy requests

To request a copy of your data, delete specific information, or exercise other privacy rights, email hi@lnkdrp.com as described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Enterprise

For share links on your own domain, single sign-on, unlimited seats and a data processing agreement, use **Talk to us** on the [Pricing](/pricing) page. Enterprise is priced on seats and volume.
