## Where the settings live

Open **Dashboard**, then **Notifications**. Each setting is yours alone and applies to the current workspace only.

Every email kind has three choices:

- **Off**: nothing is sent. Everything is still recorded in the app.
- **Daily digest**: one email at the end of the day (UTC). Nothing arrives on a day with no activity.
- **Immediately**: within about five minutes.

## When someone opens a link

Sent when a recipient opens one of the workspace's share links for the first time. Your own opens never count. The default is a daily digest.

The email says which link was opened and when, and links straight to that reader's activity. On Pro it also names the reader when known, with the pages reached and time spent. On Free it does not name anyone.

## Turning view emails off from the email

Every view email ends with **Turn off these emails** and **Change how often**. The off link works without signing in, only affects your view emails for that workspace, and can be undone from the Notifications page.

## Other email kinds

- **Doc update emails**: someone replaced a document and the comparison found real changes.
- **New documents from teammates**: someone else in the workspace uploaded a document. A workspace with a single member never sends these.

## Slack

The same moments can post to a Slack channel: a first open, a visit brief, a replaced document, a received file and a document added to a project, each with a link back. Connect a channel from Integrations in the sidebar; see [Slack](/help/slack).

## The activity feed

**Activity** in the sidebar lists everything that happens in the workspace, grouped by day and filterable by type: uploads, link changes, opens, downloads, members and agent actions. On Free, viewer names are withheld, as on the metrics page.

## Visit briefs

A visit brief is a short written account of one reader's visit: what held them, what they skipped, how long they stayed, whether they came back and how it compares with their last visit. It is written by the AI from the record of the visit, never from anything the reader typed.

- **When it arrives**: a few minutes after the reader stops. A visit is considered over once it has been quiet for a couple of minutes, so a closed tab is briefed within about seven minutes; a tab left open takes a few minutes longer.
- **Pro only**. Free workspaces get a line in their open email instead.
- **1 credit** per brief, on every quality level. There is no level to choose.
- **Not every visit earns one.** A glance, under twenty seconds on one page, is not written up and sends nothing immediately; it shows in the daily digest. Your own opens are never briefed.
- Briefs are stored, so they also appear as a row in the activity feed.

### The brief email

The setting is **When someone finishes reading**, with the choices Off, Daily digest and **After each visit** (the default). The subject line is the brief's headline. The body covers who read it, through which link, for how long, the brief itself, what caught their attention, time per page, the reading path and the pages they skipped, with a link to the reader's page.

With both kinds on immediate you get two emails per first visit: the open email, then the brief minutes after the close. For one email per visit, keep the brief and turn the open email off.

### Turning briefs off

- **For yourself**: set **When someone finishes reading** to Off on the Notifications page, or use the off link in a brief email. Briefs are still written and kept on the reader's page.
- **For the whole workspace**: turn off automatic briefs under **AI defaults** in the dashboard. No credits are spent and nothing is written. You still receive an email with the facts of the visit.

### When credits run out

You still get the email with the facts of the visit: pages, times and return count. The write-up is skipped, the email says so, and the Notifications page shows a **Paused** note next to the setting. Briefs resume when you add credits. See [Credits and plans](/help/credits-and-plans).
