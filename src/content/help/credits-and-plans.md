## Free and Pro

Plans are per workspace. Upgrade a workspace once and everything in it is on Pro.

**Free**, $0 forever:

- 10 shared documents, each with unlimited share links. A document counts while sharing is on; archive it or turn sharing off to free the slot.
- 2 projects.
- Basic analytics: totals and views by day for the last 7 days, without viewer identities.
- Password protection and download control.
- Single user. One team workspace in addition to your personal one.
- 100 credits to start, one time, and at most 15 credits a day.

**Pro**, per workspace, price shown at checkout:

- Unlimited documents, share links and projects.
- Deep analytics: who opened it, time per page, return visits, full history.
- A visit brief after every finished visit.
- Recipients can browse every version and see what changed.
- 500 credits a month.
- 3 teammates beyond the owner who can upload and share, plus unlimited free viewers.
- Agents never take a seat.

See [Pricing](/pricing) for the full comparison.

## What credits pay for

Credits are only spent when the AI reads a document. Uploads, replacements, links, passwords, downloads and analytics never cost credits.

| Per run | Basic | Standard | Advanced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Summary and key points | 1 | 2 | 5 |
| AI compare (what changed between versions) | 2 | 5 | 12 |
| Visit brief | 1 | 1 | 1 |

- The summary runs automatically at Basic on every upload. Ask for a better one from the document page.
- The summary costs 0 credits when your own agent writes it over MCP, and for files recipients upload through a replace link.
- AI compare runs automatically when you replace a PDF: at Basic on Free, at Standard on Pro. You can change the default level, or run it by hand from the version history page.

**Quality levels**: Basic is a quick pass for short documents and everyday changes. Standard reads more carefully. Advanced is the deepest reading, for long or high-stakes documents.

See [/costs](/costs) for the full table. Every run is listed on the dashboard Usage tab.

## Where credits come from

- **Free**: 100 starter credits, once. There is no monthly top-up. When they run out, buy a credit pack at [/credits](/credits), from $7, or upgrade. Purchased credits do not expire.
- **Pro**: 500 credits each billing cycle, reset on your renewal date. Unused credits do not roll over.
- **On-demand (Pro only)**: extra credits at $0.10 each, billed through Stripe, under a hard spend limit you set on the dashboard Limits page. A limit of 0 turns on-demand off.

## What happens at zero credits

Uploads still complete and links keep working. The AI runs pause:

- The automatic summary is skipped. You can write it later from the document page for 1 credit.
- AI compare is skipped; the version is still recorded and you can compare later.
- Visit briefs become a plain recap of the visit without the write-up.

## Going over a Free limit

Existing links never stop working. At the document cap, a new document is created with sharing off until you archive another or upgrade. A workspace that was already over a Free limit when limits were introduced, or that drops from Pro to Free, gets a 14-day grace period with reminder emails before new documents and projects are blocked.

## Upgrading and billing

- Upgrade from [Pricing](/pricing) or the **Upgrade** button on the dashboard. Checkout is through Stripe.
- **Manage subscription** on the dashboard opens the Stripe billing portal for invoices and cancellation.
- Cancel any time. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period, then the workspace returns to Free.
- Each workspace is billed separately.

Pricing change, September 13, 2026: the automatic AI summary now costs 1 credit. It was previously included. Starter credits already granted were kept.
